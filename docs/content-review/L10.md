  - Resolution: content now teaches salinity. Key idea 6 says saltier seawater is denser. The O3 Explorer card says forming sea ice leaves its salt behind, so polar water becomes colder, saltier and denser. Q18 keys both "chilled by frigid air" and "becomes saltier as sea ice forms".
