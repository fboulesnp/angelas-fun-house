## Objectives
- L21-O1 (observe): recognize predation and sort predators into carnivores, herbivores and
  omnivores by what they mainly eat, including why plant eaters count as predators here. Families
  F01, F02, F08 (5 questions).
- L21-O2 (observe): classify an interaction as mutualism, commensalism, parasitism, predation or
  competition by rating its effect on each partner (+, −, 0). Families F02, F05, F06, F07, F08,
  F09, F11 (12 questions).
- L21-O3 (relate): explain the cause-and-effect link between predator and prey populations and
  interpret data and graphs in which predator changes trail prey changes. Families F02, F03, F04,
  F10 (7 questions).
- L21-O4 (apply): predict ripple effects of a population change, explain why repeating interaction
  patterns let scientists predict in ecosystems with different species, and use interaction patterns
  to choose management steps (Yellowstone wolves, kelp forest, ants and aphids, snow leopards).
  Families F09, F10, F11, F12, F14 (9 questions). Objective text and pages (adds p. 275) extended in
  reviser round 1 for Q29.
- L21-O5 (apply): use criteria and constraints to judge a design for monitoring a predator
  population (Yellowstone radio collars). Families F13, F14 (4 questions; at the minimum, so Q25–Q28
  must be fixed rather than rejected).
