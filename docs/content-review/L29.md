## Objectives
- L29-O1 (relate): explain how a growing population and rising per capita use combine to increase
  demand on ecosystems, using population and resource-trend graphs as evidence. Families F01, F02
  (5 questions).
- L29-O2 (observe): identify the lesson's six disruptions (over-exploitation, pollution, emerging
  disease, habitat degradation, invasive species and climate change) and recognize an example of
  each. Families F03, F04, F06, F14 (7 questions).
- L29-O3 (relate): trace cause-and-effect chains from a human change through an ecosystem and back to
  people or wildlife (overfishing → snails → bilharzia; nitrogen → algae → low oxygen; bats → pigs →
  people), and explain which ecosystem services people lose when an ecosystem is damaged. The
  ecosystem-services clause was added in round 1. Families F04, F05, F06, F13, F14 (9 questions).
- L29-O4 (apply): evaluate engineered solutions against criteria, constraints and tradeoffs.
  Families F06, F07, F08, F09, F11 (9 questions).
- L29-O5 (apply): use models and monitoring data to test whether a solution works and how to revise
  it, and explain what science can and cannot decide. Families F09, F10, F11, F12 (9 questions).
