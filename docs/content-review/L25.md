## Objectives
- L25-O1 (observe): identify where volcanoes and earthquakes tend to happen (convergent, divergent
  and transform boundaries, hot spots, faults inside plates) and the map pattern this makes, with its
  exceptions. Families F01, F02, F06 (6 questions).
- L25-O2 (relate): explain the causes: how added material, lower pressure or a rising plume lets rock
  melt; why thick, gas-rich magma erupts explosively; how stored stress becomes an earthquake; how
  seismic waves spread. Families F03, F04, F05, F06, F07 (10 questions). The learner-facing text was
  shortened in round 1: what lets rock melt, why thick, gassy magma explodes, and how stuck rock that
  suddenly slips sends out fast and slow seismic waves.
- L25-O3 (apply): use monitoring data, frequency and magnitude records and hazard maps to judge
  likelihood; explain why eruptions can be forecast from precursors but earthquakes only as chances,
  not times. Families F02, F08, F09, F10, F11 (9 questions). The learner-facing text was shortened in
  round 1: monitoring data, past records and hazard maps judge likelihood, and eruptions can be
  forecast but earthquake times cannot.
- L25-O4 (apply): define criteria and constraints, use the hazard-and-performance chart, and compare
  designs with a fair, explicit test. Families F11, F12, F13 (6 questions).
