## Review flags and resolutions
- **L07-RF1** (pp. 77, 78, 84): Earth called closed with "none" of its matter entering or leaving.
  - The same p. 78 lists exceptions, and Earth is open to energy.
  - Resolution: content says "nearly closed for matter, open to energy". Q03 separates matter from energy, and Q04 explains why the model stays useful.
  - Sources: NASA Asteroid Fast Facts (more than 100 tons of dust per day); NASA Earth Observatory atmospheric escape (several hundred tons per day); NASA Earth Fact Sheet (mass 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg).
  - Round 2: the textbook never says Earth is open to energy, so that half of the correction (glossary "closed system", key idea 1, O2 card, Q03) now cites NASA Earth Observatory "Climate and Earth's Energy Budget" (sunlight in is balanced by thermal infrared radiation out). The same record is on Q03, whose scope now includes p. 84.
- **L07-RF2** (pp. 79, 84): "water molecules a dinosaur drank", and the p. 84 spread says the total number of atoms *and molecules* never changes.
  - Atoms persist, but molecules are split in photosynthesis and rebuilt in respiration. Reactions change molecule counts: 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O → 1 C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ turns 12 molecules into 7.
  - Resolution: Q07 keys the atom-level revision, and the O2 card watchOut says the same. Key idea 2 and the O2 objective now say atoms are rearranged into new molecules (round 1 added the p. 84 claim and a p. 84 evidence record).
  - Sources: OpenStax Biology 2e 8.2 and 7.4.
- **L07-RF3** (pp. 79, 84): Figure 2 labels ice → vapor "Evaporation", which is really sublimation.
  - The spread says "water gas" and has a blue arrow labeled "Photosynthesis" between the cloud and soil boxes.
  - Resolution: Q09 keys sublimation, and the evaporation distractor explains the label. Q08 describes clouds as droplets. The spread is cited for its text boxes only. Round 1: the O1 card's first paragraph now teaches sublimation.
  - Sources: USGS Water Science School pages on sublimation and condensation.
- **L07-RF4** (p. 82): lightning's products "fall to Earth as ammonia".
  - Lightning makes nitrogen oxides, which reach the ground in rain as nitrates.
  - Resolution: no question says lightning makes ammonia. The O3 card and Q19's rationale say nitrates.
  - Sources: NASA Aura lightning NOx page; EarthDate "Lightning Strikes".
- **L07-RF5** (p. 82): "only one process … two ways" leaves out industrial (Haber–Bosch) fixation.
  - Resolution: Q19 keys fertilizer factories as a third way, and its rationale now names the textbook's two-way version first. The O3 card mentions factories.
  - Source: UF/IFAS EDIS SS684.
- **L07-RF6** (p. 83): "you use the nitrates in the peanut" to make your proteins.
